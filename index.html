A menu bar app for Mac

Lyrics on your desktop.

Play a song. Lilt brings its lyrics and album colours to the space behind your windows.

See how it works ↓ macOS 15 or later

The desktop scene

In step with the song.

01

Start with the player you already use

Play a track in Spotify or Apple Music. When timed lyrics are available, Lilt scrolls them in sync and brings the current line into focus.

02

Give the album room

Let the cover tint the desktop, use lyrics over your own wallpaper, or keep just the ambient colour. The scene sits behind your windows.

03

Keep it within reach

Open Lilt from the menu bar or press ⌃⌥L to toggle the scene. Pause for a moment and your wallpaper comes back.
