02 / On your Mac

A little more room for the music.

macOS 15 or later · Spotify and Apple Music

01

Follow the words

When timed lyrics are available, each line rises into focus as the song plays. You can adjust the timing or import an LRC file.

02

Make the scene yours

Fill the desktop with album colours, float lyrics over your wallpaper, or keep just the ambient glow.

03

Back to your wallpaper

Pause the music or press ⌃⌥L to hide the scene. Lilt lives in the menu bar, behind your regular windows.
