Who operates Lilt

Sahil Ambegaonkar operates Lilt, a macOS menu bar app, and this website. Lilt works without an account and includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

Playback on your Mac

With your macOS Automation permission, Lilt reads the current track title, artist, album, artwork, duration, playback state, and position from Spotify or Apple Music. It uses this information to show the artwork and keep lyrics in sync. Lilt never asks for your Spotify or Apple Music password.

Lyrics and artwork requests

To find synchronized lyrics, Lilt sends the track title and artist to LRCLIB, along with the album and duration when available for a match. Requests also identify the app and version. Playback position and imported lyric files stay on your Mac. LRCLIB API documentation

When Spotify supplies an HTTPS artwork URL, Lilt fetches the cover from that URL. LRCLIB and the artwork host receive the request and standard connection information, including your IP address. Each service handles its own requests under its own practices.

Local data and removal

Lilt stores your settings, downloaded lyric results, and copies of imported LRC files on your Mac. Cached matches are reused for up to 30 days and missed lookups for up to six hours. The app keeps at most 250 lookup entries. Those reuse periods do not automatically erase the cache. Imported files stay until you remove them.

To clear downloaded results or imported copies, quit Lilt and remove the relevant data under ~/Library/Caches/Lilt/ or ~/Library/Application Support/Lilt/Imported Lyrics/. For a sandboxed build, these folders may be inside Lilt’s app container.

Your choices

To stop Lilt from reading playback or making new playback-related requests, quit it from the menu bar. You can revoke player access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation → Lilt.

Website and support

This website uses static pages with no tracking scripts, analytics, or cookies set by Lilt. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process connection and request data to serve it. GitHub’s privacy statement

If you email liltwallpapers@gmail.com, Sahil receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. The information is used to reply and resolve your request. Support correspondence is kept as needed for those purposes and handled through Gmail. For privacy questions or to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, use the same address. Google’s privacy policy
